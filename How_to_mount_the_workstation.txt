In Command Prompt (without administrative permissions)

1. mount the drive through:
- "C:\Program Files\SSHFS-Win\bin\ssh.exe" ffp116@ic-s7m0kc20734.dept.ic.ac.uk
You will need to retype your user name, and then your Imperial SSH password.

2. you should now be logged in to the machine. Use command:
- exit to leave. 

3. use the following command to mount and connect back to the workstation:
- "C:\Program Files\SSHFS-Win\bin\sshfs-win.exe" svc \sshfs\%USERNAME%=ffp116@ic-s7m0kc20734.dept.ic.ac.uk X: %USERNAME% -o umask=000,create_file_umask=0117,create_dir_umask=0007,ServerAliveInterval=30
You will either be asked to enter your password or you will just see a blinking cursor. In both cases, you need to enter your password. Whatever is typed into the terminal is not hidden. The password will be visible to anyone looking. 

4. if successful, those two lines will show up:
">> OK"
">> The service sshfs has been started."
You now have READ and WRITE permission through Microsoft Explorer to work on the workstation folders.
